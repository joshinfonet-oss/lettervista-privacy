Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how LetterVista: Word Journeys (Android package com.lettervista.wordjourneys) handles information. The app is published by JoshInfotech (“we,” “us”).

1. Information handled by the app

  • Game and profile information: Level and puzzle progress, coins, gems, settings, selected avatar, player name, and profile information or photo you choose to add. Core game data is stored on your device. If you use cloud save or social features, related progress and profile data, friend codes, friendships, and friend requests are processed through Firebase.
  • Account information: Firebase creates an anonymous app account for app services. If you choose to link an email and password, Firebase processes the email address and authentication credentials. The app does not receive or store your password.
  • Words submitted to the shared word bank: A meaningful word accepted as an Extra Word may be sent to our Supabase word-bank service. The service checks submissions against its validation and moderation rules. Approved words may be retained in the shared word bank and considered as candidate puzzle words. The shared word entries are not stored with your Firebase user ID. A separate daily submission-limit record uses a SHA-256 hash of the Firebase user ID; that account-linked quota record is removed when the account is deleted.
  • Advertising information: LetterVista uses Google Mobile Ads to provide rewarded video ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address or approximate location, ad interactions, and related technical information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising, subject to Google’s terms and the choices available in your region and device.
  • Purchase information: Optional in-game purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment methods. LetterVista receives product and purchase-status information needed to grant items and may process purchase records associated with the app account. LetterVista does not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Service and diagnostic information: Firebase, Supabase, Google Play Billing, and Google Mobile Ads may process technical logs, device/app information, and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services. Each provider handles information under its own privacy terms.

2. How information is used

We use information to run the game, save and restore progress, provide cloud and social features, validate and moderate submitted words, maintain the shared word bank, grant purchases and rewards, show rewarded ads, prevent abuse, and maintain and secure the app.

3. Storage and service providers

Game data is stored locally on your device. When you use cloud, account, or social features, relevant information is processed by Firebase and Google. Word submissions and the hashed daily quota record are processed by Supabase. Google Mobile Ads processes information needed for rewarded advertising, and Google Play processes billing transactions. We do not sell personal information.

Approved shared-word entries may remain available as candidate words after account deletion because they are not stored with your account identity. Do not enter personal information into word or other game fields.

4. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s data, uninstall the app, or use the in-app account deletion option. When account deletion completes, LetterVista deletes the Firebase Authentication account and the account-linked cloud records handled by its deletion flow, clears the app’s local data on that device, and requests removal of the account-linked Supabase quota record. Approved words already placed in the shared word bank may remain in de-identified form. Service providers may retain limited copies in backups or where required for security, legal, or operational reasons, under their retention practices.

Request account deletion: To delete an account in the app, open Settings → Delete account and confirm. If you cannot access LetterVista, email joshinfonet@gmail.com with the subject “LetterVista account deletion.” Include the account email, if one is linked, or the player ID shown in the app so we can locate the account. Do not send your password. We will verify the request and delete the Firebase account and associated account-linked cloud data. Approved shared words may remain without account identity; the hashed word-bank quota record is removed.

5. Children’s privacy

LetterVista is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us to request review and deletion. If the app’s audience, content, or promotion changes, we will review the applicable child-safety and advertising requirements.

6. Security

We use the security features provided by our service providers and take reasonable steps to protect information. Information sent to Firebase, Supabase, Google Mobile Ads, or Google Play is transmitted using the secure connections supported by those services. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You can play using device storage, choose whether to link an email account for cloud features, choose whether to watch a rewarded ad, and request account deletion using the in-app or email method above. You can manage advertising choices through controls presented by the app, your device, or Google where available. For privacy questions or other data requests, contact us using the address below.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information on servers outside your country. They apply their own safeguards and privacy practices for international processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will change the “Last updated” date above and provide notice where required.
