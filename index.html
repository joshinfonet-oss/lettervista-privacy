This policy explains how LetterVista (Android package com.lettervista.wordjourneys) handles information when you play. The app is published by JoshInfotech (“we,” “us”).

1. Information the app handles

  • Game and profile information: levels, puzzle progress, coins and gems, settings, selected avatar, and profile details or photo that you choose to add. Core game state is stored on your device. If you sign in or link an account and use cloud save, progress/profile data may also be synced through Firebase.
  • Account information: Firebase creates an anonymous app account automatically for app features. If you choose to link/sign in using email and password, Firebase processes your email address and account credentials. We do not receive or store your password in LetterVista.
  • Words you submit: When you enter a meaningful word that is accepted as an Extra Word, the app may queue and send it to our Supabase word-bank service. Only words accepted by the app’s dictionary and blocklist checks are submitted. The service rejects invalid, blocked, duplicate, or unsupported words. Approved words are added to a shared word bank for possible puzzle use. The shared word entry is not stored with your Firebase user ID. A separate daily quota record uses a SHA-256 hash of the Firebase user ID to enforce submission limits.
  • Advertising information: The app uses Google Mobile Ads for rewarded video ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device and ad identifiers, ad interaction, approximate diagnostics, and other information described in Google’s own privacy materials, for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes. Ad availability and personalization may depend on your region and consent choices.
  • Purchases: Optional coin and gem purchases use Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payment details; LetterVista receives purchase/product status needed to grant in-game items. The app does not receive your full payment-card number. Purchase verification is currently not configured on our server in the reviewed build.
  • Technical and service data: Firebase, Supabase, Google Play Billing, and Google Mobile Ads may process service logs, app/device information, and diagnostic data required to provide, secure, and operate their services. Their handling is governed by their respective privacy terms.

2. How information is used

We use this information to save and restore game progress, provide account and social/cloud features you choose to use, validate and moderate submitted words, supply shared puzzle words, grant purchases and rewards, show rewarded ads, prevent abuse, and maintain the app and its services.

3. Where information is stored and who receives it

Game state is stored locally on your device. When you use cloud features, relevant account and game data is processed by Firebase/Google. Submitted approved words and quota records are processed by Supabase. Google Mobile Ads and Google Play receive information necessary for advertising and billing. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Approved shared-word entries are intended to be available as candidate words for puzzles and are not tied to a player identity in the shared-word table. Please do not submit personal information in a word or other app field.

4. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s data, uninstall the app, or delete it through an available in-app reset option. Cloud data is retained while needed to provide cloud features and is deleted when a valid deletion request is completed, subject to operational, legal, security, and backup-retention needs. Approved words may remain in the shared word bank after account deletion because they are not stored there with your account identity.

Important limitation: In the reviewed build, the in-app “Delete account” option clears local app storage only. It does not delete the Firebase Authentication account or associated cloud records. To request deletion of cloud account data, email joshinfonet@gmail.com with the subject “LetterVista account deletion.” We will need to verify the request and remove the Firebase account and associated personal cloud data. The in-app option must be updated to delete cloud data as well before this app is submitted to Google Play.

5. Children’s privacy

LetterVista is a general-audience word puzzle and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We recommend listing the app for ages 13 and older, provided the app content, artwork, store listing, and promotion are not designed to attract children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request. If the app is marketed to children or selected for a child-inclusive audience, additional child-protection, advertising, age-screening, and parental-consent requirements may apply.

6. Security

We and our service providers use administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your account credentials private and use only official app stores to install LetterVista.

7. Your choices

You can play using local device storage, choose whether to link an account for cloud features, and choose whether to watch a rewarded ad. You may manage advertising and consent choices through the options presented in the app or your device settings where available. You can request access to or deletion of personal information using the contact details below, subject to applicable law and verification.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information on servers in countries other than your own. Where required, they use safeguards for cross-border transfers as described in their privacy documentation.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the effective date above and provide notice in the app or through the app listing when required.
